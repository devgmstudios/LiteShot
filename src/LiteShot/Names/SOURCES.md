# Name list sources

`first-names.txt`, `first-names-ambiguous.txt` and `surnames.txt` are generated by
`tools/names/build-name-lists.mjs` (`node tools/names/build-name-lists.mjs --download`). They hold names only, no
people: LiteShot uses them on the user's PC to recognise names on screen so it can blur them.

| Data | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US first names | U.S. Social Security Administration baby names, top 1,000 per year and sex, 1880–2008 (mirror: github.com/hadley/data-baby-names) | Public domain (U.S. government work) |
| US surnames | U.S. Census Bureau, 2010 Census surnames with 500+ people | Public domain (U.S. government work) |
| Given and family names from the US, UK, Ireland, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, the Nordic countries and the rest of Europe | Wikidata, via the QLever endpoint: names held by at least 10 notable people who are citizens of those countries | CC0 |
| Filters only (not shipped) | Hunspell en_US and en_GB dictionaries (github.com/wooorm/dictionaries); Wikidata places, brands, nationalities and languages | Used at build time to leave out everyday words, places, brands and nationalities |
