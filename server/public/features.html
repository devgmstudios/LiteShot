Everything LiteShot does.

New in 1.1.6 Easy to see

Bright mode.

For anyone who finds the glass toolbars hard to read. Solid black, bigger white icons, bright yellow for whatever is turned on, and a name on every button so there's no need to hover. Try the switch.

  • Two switchesSettings › Capture › Easy to see. Turning on Bright mode turns on the names too.
  • Follows WindowsHigh contrast themes, reduced transparency and reduced motion are respected.
  • Keys for everythingEvery tool and action has a shortcut, shown when you hover.

Capture

Two clicks, any screen.

PrtScn freezes every monitor and dims it. Click a window or drag an area, and the tools appear beside it.

LiteShotcapture screen

Snaps to windows

Hover a window and click once to capture exactly that window.

Scrolling capture S

LiteShot scrolls a long page by itself and stitches it into one tall image, even web apps with a side menu.

Last area again

Ctrl PrtScn reopens the area you used last. Shift PrtScn saves the full screen.

Pixel loupe

Coordinates and the hex color under your cursor. Press C to copy the color.

Every monitor

Multiple screens and mixed scaling, pixel-exact.

The pointer, if you want it

Draw the mouse pointer into screenshots with one switch.

Mark up

Point at what matters.

Every tool has a one-letter key, and anything you draw can be clicked to move, recolor or resize later.

Drawing tools

Pen P, line L, arrow A, box R, circle E and highlighter M.

Numbered steps N

Click, click, click: 1, 2, 3 for instructions anyone can follow.

Text that looks good T

Ten built-in fonts or your own, bold, italic, alignment, and plain, outline, label or shadow looks.

Color and size

Thirteen colors plus any color you like, and five sizes for every tool.

Change your mind

Move, recolor or resize any drawing. Undo and redo with Ctrl Z and Y.

Never tinted

Toolbars float beside your capture; the screenshot itself is never dimmed or blurred.

Privacy

Personal info hides itself.

Switch on Hide personal info and LiteShot blurs what's personal the moment you capture. It all runs on your PC.

LiteShotHide personal info

Hide personal info H

Names, emails, phone and card numbers are blurred on every capture until you switch it off.

Your keywords

Client names, fund names, project codes: add them once and they're hidden every time.

Blur faces F

Every face gets an oval Shield blur by FacePrivacy.ai, side profiles too.

Blur brush U

Paint blur over anything else, or pixelate it with B.

Unblur just one

Click a blur and press Delete. Press H again to show everything.

Nothing uploaded

Windows' own text recognition finds them, on your PC.

Share and save

Links that delete themselves.

Copy it, save it, or share a private link. Uploads are encrypted on your PC first, so nobody else, us included, can open them.

Copy Ctrl C

Straight to the clipboard, and the overlay closes.

Save Ctrl S

PNG, JPG or BMP, with your own file name pattern, or save copies on their own.

Share

Post the private link to X, Facebook, Reddit, Pinterest or VK, or email it.

Search similar Ctrl K

Find where an image appears on the web, with Google Lens.

Print Ctrl P

Right from the editor.

Delete sooner

Every link you made is in the tray menu, ready to delete early.

AI

Edit with AI, or make it move.

Describe a change, like "remove the background", or turn the screenshot into a short video. Only when you ask: nothing is sent otherwise.

LiteShotEdit with AI

Edit with AI

FLUX and Nano Banana redraw the screenshot the way you describe it.

Make a video

Seedance and Kling bring the screenshot to life in a few seconds of video.

Your own SyncNode key

AI runs through your SyncNode account, so you pay only for what you use and there's nothing to sign up for with us.

Pins and text

Keep it in view. Copy what it says.

Float a screenshot above every window while you work, and pull the text out of any capture.

Pin to screen Ctrl T

It stays on top of everything. Drag it anywhere.

Resize and zoom

Drag an edge or corner, or scroll to zoom. It keeps its shape.

Fade and click through

Ctrl + scroll fades it; T lets clicks pass through to the window underneath.

Turn it around

Rotate with R and L, flip with H and V, nudge with the arrow keys.

Copy text Ctrl Shift C

Pull the text out of a screenshot or a pin with the recognition built into Windows. Works offline.
