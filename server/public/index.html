Privacy

Names blur themselves.

Turn on Blur names automatically and LiteShot reads the screen the moment you capture, finds anything that looks like a person's name and blurs it. Drag the slider to compare.

  • Runs on your PCWindows' own text recognition, so nothing is uploaded to find them.
  • Your callCtrl+Z brings them all back; click one blur and press Delete to unblur just that name.
  • Faces too, by FacePrivacy.aiPress F and every face in the selection gets an oval blur, or press U and paint with FacePrivacy's blur brush.

Features

Everything Lightshot did.
Then a lot more.

The same muscle memory: press PrtScn, drag, copy. Everything else is new.

Mark it up in seconds

Pen, line, arrow, box, circle, highlighter, numbered steps and text with ten built-in fonts — or your own. Click any drawing to move it, recolor it or resize it.

Snaps to windows

Hover a window and click once to capture exactly that window. Ctrl+PrtScn reopens the area you used last.

Private by default

Names are blurred the moment you capture. Blur faces, with technology from FacePrivacy.ai, hides every face with one key — or paint over anything with their blur brush. It all runs on your PC.

Edit with AI, or make it move

Describe a change — "remove the background" — or turn the screenshot into a short video. Powered by FLUX, Nano Banana, Seedance and Kling through your SyncNode key.

Links that delete themselves

Uploads are encrypted on your PC before they leave it and hosted on fortefile.com. The key lives only in your link, so nobody else — us included — can open the file, and it's gone after 24 hours. Delete it sooner from the tray menu.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, and open source under the MIT license. No account, no trial, no ads. AI features use your own SyncNode key, so you pay SyncNode and Replicate for what you use.

Does LiteShot upload my screenshots?

Only when you press Upload or Share. Name and face blurring, text recognition and everything else run on your PC, and uploads are encrypted before they leave it. The one exception is Search similar images: Google has to see the picture, so that upload isn't encrypted and is deleted after an hour.

Who can open my links?

Anyone you send the link to, until it expires. The decryption key is the part after the # in the link; browsers never send it to the server, so fortefile.com only ever stores scrambled data.

Do I need to install .NET?

No. Both the installer and the portable exe include everything LiteShot needs.

How do updates work?

Check for updates in Settings › General, or turn on automatic updates. Every release is signed by Devgm, LLC, and LiteShot refuses anything that isn't.

Is this Lightshot?

No. LiteShot is an independent app by Devgm, LLC, inspired by the classic two-click workflow. It isn't affiliated with Lightshot or Skillbrains.
