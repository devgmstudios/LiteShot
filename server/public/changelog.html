Changelog

What's new.

Every LiteShot release, newest first. LiteShot updates itself if you turn on automatic updates in Settings › General.

Mac 0.3.0

BetaNovember 15, 2026
  • The Mac app catches up with Windows. Ask AI (⇧⌘A), Edit with AI and Make a video; encrypted private links (⌘D), Share and Search similar images (⌘K), with Recent Uploads in the menu bar; scrolling capture (S); text styling with the same seven fonts and four looks; click a drawing to move, recolor or delete it; the pixel loupe (C copies the color); Print (⌘P); and pins that rotate, flip, let clicks through and copy their text.
  • A Settings window (⌘, in the menu bar) for the SyncNode key (kept in your Keychain), the AI models, Bright mode, uploads and updates.
  • Signed updates. From this version the Mac app updates itself, only with releases signed by Devgm.

1.1.9

LatestNovember 14, 2026
  • Ask AI. Press Ctrl+Shift+A (or Ask AI in the toolbar) to chat about your screenshot: what's in it, summarize it, explain an error, translate it, draft a reply, and keep asking follow-ups. It uses your SyncNode key with OpenRouter; pick the model under Settings › Cloud & AI (Claude Sonnet 5.5 by default, or Claude Haiku, Claude Fable, GPT, Gemini, Grok or any OpenRouter model that reads images).

1.1.8

November 14, 2026
  • Related words in Find on screen. When the word you type isn't on screen, related ones light up in blue instead: "fish" finds salmon, trout and tuna, "car" finds a sedan or a taxi. The word list is built in, so it works offline (Open English WordNet, CC BY 4.0).

Mac 0.2.0

BetaNovember 14, 2026
  • Find on screen on Mac. Press ⌘F and type: every place the text appears lights up, Return steps through the matches, Highlight marks them all, and related words light up when the word itself isn't there.

1.1.7

November 14, 2026
  • Find on screen. Press Ctrl+F (or Find in the toolbar) and type: every place the text appears in the selection lights up while the rest dims. Enter and Shift+Enter step through the matches, and Highlight marks them all with the highlighter. It reads the screen with Windows' own text recognition, offline. With nothing selected, it searches the whole screen.

Mac 0.1.0

BetaNovember 3, 2026
  • LiteShot for Mac. A native menu bar app for macOS 14 and later, on Apple silicon and Intel. Press ⌘⇧9 to capture a window or an area, mark it up, hide personal info and faces, copy the text, copy, save or pin it. Bright mode and button names are in the menu. Download the beta.

1.1.6

November 2, 2026
  • Bright mode. Solid black toolbars with bigger white icons, and bright yellow for whatever is turned on. Easier to see on any screen. Settings › Capture › Easy to see.
  • Button names. A short name on every toolbar button, so there's no need to hover to find out what it does.

1.1.5

October 31, 2026
  • Count this copy. Once a day the update check includes a random number, so we can count how many copies of LiteShot are in use. Nothing about you or your PC is sent, and you can switch it off under Settings › Updates. See everything LiteShot sends.

1.1.4

October 31, 2026
  • Scrolling capture works on web apps. Pages with a fixed side menu, a details panel or their own scrollbar now stitch all the way down, and the side menu shows once instead of repeating.
  • Resize pins. Drag any edge or corner of a pinned screenshot to make it bigger or smaller; it keeps its shape.
  • More for pins: rotate (R, L) and flip (H, V), Copy text (Ctrl+Shift+C), arrow keys to nudge, and click-through (T) so you can work under a pin. Make pins clickable again from the tray menu.

1.1.3

October 30, 2026
  • Scrolling capture (S, or Scrolling capture in the tray menu): LiteShot scrolls a long page by itself and stitches it into one tall image you can edit like any other.
  • Better face blur. Finds many more small faces and faces on big multi-monitor captures, and now blurs side profiles too.
  • Better name hiding. Knows tens of thousands more names from the US, UK, Europe and the Nordics, catches ALL-CAPS names with middle initials, and names next to words like "emailed" or "Hi", while leaving everyday words alone.
  • Update status. Settings shows your version and whether you're up to date, with a Check now button for updating by hand.

1.1.2

October 28, 2026
  • Hide personal info. Press H in the editor (or switch it on in Settings) and names, faces, email addresses, phone, card and social security numbers are blurred automatically — all on your PC. It stays on until you switch it off, and anything it misses you can paint over with the blur brush.
  • Your own keywords. Add client names, project codes or anything else under Settings › Capture, and they're hidden on every capture.
  • SyncNode key status. Settings shows whether a key is saved (ending in …) and lets you remove it.
  • A clearer face-blur icon.

1.1.1

October 28, 2026
  • Start with Windows and Change shortcuts… are in the tray menu.
  • LiteShot starts with Windows by default the first time it runs; the startup entry follows the exe if you move it.
  • Blur brush by FacePrivacy.ai (U): paint over a face or anything else to blur it.

1.1.0

October 28, 2026
  • First public release: the two-click screenshot tool rebuilt with a Liquid Glass interface.
  • Pen, line, arrow, box, circle, highlighter, numbered steps, text with ten fonts, pixelate.
  • Names blurred automatically and faces blurred with one key (F), all on your PC.
  • Encrypted links on fortefile.com that delete themselves after 24 hours.
  • Edit with AI or turn a screenshot into a video, through your SyncNode key.
  • Copy text (OCR), pin to screen, window capture, signed automatic updates, installer and portable exe.
