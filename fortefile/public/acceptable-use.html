Acceptable Use Policy

fortefile is for sharing files privately, for a short time. These rules keep it safe for everyone and keep it running. They are part of our Terms of Service.

Who this applies to

This policy applies to everyone who uploads, shares or opens files on fortefile, whether through LiteShot, another app that uses our interface, or the website. Breaking it can lead to the removal of files, blocked access and, where the law requires or the harm is serious, reports to the authorities.

Prohibited content

Don't upload, share or link to:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM). Any content that sexually exploits or endangers minors, including drawn, computer-generated or AI-generated imagery, and content that grooms or sexualizes children. We have zero tolerance: we remove it, block the networks involved where we can, preserve it as the law requires, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), which works with law enforcement worldwide.
  • Malware and phishing. Viruses, ransomware, spyware or other code designed to damage, disrupt or gain unauthorized access to devices, systems or data; and phishing pages, kits or files designed to steal passwords, payment details or personal information.
  • Illegal content. Content that is illegal under U.S. law or where you or your recipient are, including content that facilitates the sale of illegal goods or services, stolen data, fraud or scams.
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery. Sexual or intimate images of a real person shared without their consent, including fakes and deepfakes.
  • Violent threats and extremism. Content that threatens or incites violence, or promotes terrorist or violent extremist organizations.
  • Harassment and privacy violations. Content used to harass, bully, intimidate, stalk or dox anyone, or that exposes someone's private information, such as identity documents, financial details or home address, without permission.
  • Infringement. Content that infringes copyright, trademarks or other intellectual property or privacy rights. Copyright owners can use our DMCA procedure.

Abuse of the service

Don't:

  • get around our limits on file size, lifetime or upload rate, or any block we've applied, for example by rotating IP addresses or splitting files;
  • use fortefile as a general file host, content delivery network or back end for another product, for example by embedding files in websites or apps, or by uploading in bulk with automated tools (personal tools that follow these rules and encrypt files the way LiteShot does are fine; contact us before using fortefile in a product or at scale);
  • use fortefile to deliver attacks, to control malware or to move stolen data;
  • use the public search-image upload for anything other than a screenshot sent to a reverse image search;
  • try to open files you weren't given a link to, guess or enumerate file IDs, or interfere with other people's files;
  • attack the Service, overload it, or probe and test its security outside our responsible disclosure rules;
  • impersonate Devgm, fortefile or LiteShot, or use fortefile links to mislead people about where content comes from;
  • resell or sublicense the Service.

How we enforce

Encrypted files can't be read by us, so we don't scan or moderate them in advance, and we won't weaken the encryption to do so. Instead we act on reports that include the full link, on legal notices, and on signals we can see without any key, such as the volume, size and rate of uploads from a network.

When we find a violation, we may, at our discretion and without notice:

  • delete files or block access to links;
  • block uploads or downloads from IP addresses or networks;
  • report the matter to NCMEC, law enforcement or other authorities;
  • preserve information where the law requires it, and respond to valid legal process as described in our Privacy Policy.

We may also act against content or behavior not listed here if we believe it is harmful. We don't have to warn anyone first. If you think we made a mistake, write to abuse@fortefile.com with the link and why, and we'll take another look. Deleted files can't be restored.

How to report

Email abuse@fortefile.com with:

  1. The full fortefile link, including everything after the “#”. Without the key we can't see the file. We use the key only to review the file you reported and act on it.
  2. What the problem is, and where you found the link if that helps.
  3. How to reach you, if you'd like a reply. You may also report anonymously.

Files expire within 24 hours, so please report promptly. We review reports as quickly as we can.

  • Child sexual abuse material: don't download, copy, screenshot or forward the material itself; send us only the link. You can also report directly to NCMEC's CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org, or, outside the United States, to a national hotline listed by INHOPE.
  • Someone in immediate danger: contact your local emergency services first.
  • Copyright: follow our Copyright and DMCA Policy.
  • Security vulnerabilities: see our responsible disclosure policy.

Contact

Abuse reports: abuse@fortefile.com. Other legal questions: legal@fortefile.com. fortefile is operated by Devgm, LLC.
