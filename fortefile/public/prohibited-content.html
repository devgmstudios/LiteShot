Who this applies to

These rules apply to every file on fortefile, encrypted or not, whether you share the link with one person or many, and to links that point to prohibited content somewhere else. Encryption means we usually can't see what you upload; it doesn't make prohibited content allowed. You're responsible for what you upload and share. In places this policy goes further than the law.

Child sexual exploitation

No child sexual abuse material, in any form, including drawn, computer-generated and AI-generated imagery, and nothing that sexualizes, grooms or exploits minors. We have zero tolerance: we remove it, report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), preserve it as the law requires and block the networks involved. Our Child Safety Policy has the details and explains how to report it safely.

Intimate images without consent

Don't share sexual or intimate images of a real person without their consent. That includes:

  • images shared to embarrass, punish or control someone, sometimes called “revenge porn”;
  • images taken secretly or without consent, such as hidden-camera or “upskirt” images;
  • fake or AI-generated intimate images (“deepfakes”) of a real, identifiable person;
  • threats to share intimate images to get money, more images or anything else (sextortion).

If you're shown in an intimate image shared on fortefile without your consent, email abuse@fortefile.com with the link, your name and a short statement that the image shows you (or someone you're authorized to act for) and was shared without consent. We act on valid requests within 48 hours, and usually much sooner. StopNCII.org can also help stop intimate images of adults being shared on other platforms.

Sexual content

Sexually explicit content that involves only adults is allowed only when everyone shown is an adult who agreed to it being made and shared, and you share it privately with people who chose to receive it. Don't post links to sexually explicit content publicly, send it to people who haven't agreed to see it, or use fortefile to sell, advertise or distribute adult content. Sexual content involving violence or coercion, anyone who can't or didn't consent, or animals, and content presented as involving a minor, is never allowed.

Violence, terrorism and hate

  • Terrorism and violent extremism. Content that supports, recruits for, raises money for or glorifies terrorist or violent extremist groups or their attacks, including attackers' manifestos and attack footage shared to promote them.
  • Threats and incitement. Threats of violence against a person or group, and content that encourages others to commit violence.
  • Hate. Content that attacks or dehumanizes people, or incites hatred or violence against them, because of their race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or serious illness.
  • Graphic violence and cruelty. Gore, torture or cruelty to animals shared to shock, harass or glorify.

News, documentary, research and educational material is fine when it's shared for that purpose.

Suicide and self-harm

Don't share content that encourages, instructs or glorifies suicide, self-harm or eating disorders, or that pressures someone to hurt themselves. Content about recovery, prevention and support is welcome. If you're struggling, you're not alone: in the United States, call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, and elsewhere find a helpline at findahelpline.com.

Harassment and private information

  • Content used to harass, bully, intimidate, stalk or shame someone.
  • Doxxing: sharing someone's home address, phone number, identity documents, financial or medical details, private messages or other private information without permission, or to target them.
  • Photos or recordings of people in private places taken without their knowledge.
  • Impersonating a real person to deceive or harm others.

Malware, phishing and stolen data

  • Malware: viruses, ransomware, spyware, stalkerware, trojans or other code designed to damage devices or to get into systems or data without permission.
  • Phishing: pages, kits or files that collect passwords, payment details or personal information under false pretenses.
  • Stolen data: leaked or hacked databases, passwords, session cookies, card numbers or other people's personal data.
  • Attack tools shared to attack systems you aren't authorized to test. Security research shared responsibly, for example with the affected vendor or under our disclosure rules, is fine.

Fraud and scams

  • Scams and schemes that trick people out of money, data or access to their accounts.
  • Forged or fake documents made to deceive, such as IDs, passports, bank statements, invoices, receipts, certificates or doctor's notes.
  • Counterfeit goods, and cracked or pirated software and license keys.
  • Spam: links sent in bulk to people who didn't ask for them.
  • Content that impersonates a company, government or brand to mislead people.

Illegal goods and dangerous instructions

  • Selling or arranging illegal drugs, weapons, explosives or other regulated goods without the licenses the law requires.
  • Instructions for making explosives or chemical, biological, radiological or nuclear weapons, or for causing mass harm.
  • Human trafficking, forced labor or commercial sexual exploitation.
  • Anything else that's illegal under U.S. law or where you or the person you share with are, or that breaks U.S. sanctions.

Other people's rights

Don't share content that infringes someone else's copyright, trademark or other rights, such as films, music, software, books or paid courses you don't have the right to share. Rights holders can use our Copyright and DMCA Policy.

If you break these rules

We may delete the content, block the link, block the networks involved and, where the law requires it or the harm is serious, report it to NCMEC, law enforcement or other authorities. We don't have to warn anyone first. Our Acceptable Use Policy explains how we enforce our rules and how to ask us to take another look.

How to report

Email abuse@fortefile.com with the full fortefile link, including everything after the “#”, and tell us what's wrong. You can report anonymously. For child sexual abuse material, follow the child safety reporting steps: send the link only, never the material. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.
