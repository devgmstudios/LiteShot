Our approach

fortefile files are short-lived: every one is deleted within 24 hours, and most are encrypted so that we can't see what they contain. We remove material when we receive a valid notice, and we remove it quickly.

A fortefile link has two parts: fortefile.com/f/{file ID}#{key}. The part before the # is enough for us to find and remove a file. If you include the whole link, with the key after the #, we can also confirm what we are removing. Because files expire quickly, please send notices as soon as you can.

Sending a notice of infringement

If you believe material on fortefile infringes a copyright that you own or are authorized to act for, send a written notice to our designated agent (below) that includes:

  1. your physical or electronic signature (typing your full name is enough);
  2. identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed, or, for several works, a representative list;
  3. the fortefile link or links to the material, ideally the full link including the part after the “#”;
  4. your name, postal address, telephone number and email address;
  5. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law (having considered whether the use may be fair use); and
  6. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

A notice that is missing these elements may not be valid, and we may ask you to complete it. For trademark or other complaints that aren't about copyright, write to legal@fortefile.com or abuse@fortefile.com.

What happens next

When we receive a valid notice, we expeditiously remove the material or disable access to it. Because fortefile has no accounts, we usually have no way to tell the person who uploaded it. We may block uploads from networks involved in repeated infringement.

Counter-notices

If you believe material was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, you can send our designated agent a counter-notice that includes:

  1. your physical or electronic signature;
  2. identification of the material that was removed or disabled, and the fortefile link where it appeared before;
  3. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification; and
  4. your name, postal address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the judicial district where your address is (or, if your address is outside the United States, any judicial district in which Devgm may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice, or their agent.

When we receive a valid counter-notice, we send a copy to the person who sent the original notice. Unless they tell us within 10 business days that they have filed a court action to stop the infringement, we restore the material, or stop blocking it, within 10 to 14 business days of receiving the counter-notice, where that is possible. Because fortefile deletes every file within 24 hours and doesn't keep copies, a removed file usually can't be restored; you can upload it again and share a new link.

Repeat infringers

In appropriate circumstances, we cut off people who repeatedly infringe. Since fortefile has no accounts, we do this by blocking uploads from the IP addresses, networks or apps associated with repeated infringement, wherever we can identify them.

False claims

Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), anyone who knowingly and materially misrepresents that material is infringing, or that it was removed by mistake, may be liable for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees. If you aren't sure whether material infringes your rights, consider talking to a lawyer before sending a notice.

Designated agent

Send notices and counter-notices to our designated copyright agent:

Copyright Agent, Devgm, LLC (fortefile) Email: legal@fortefile.com
Subject line: “DMCA notice” or “DMCA counter-notice”

Email is the fastest way to reach us. If you need to send your notice by post, email us first and we'll give you a postal address.
