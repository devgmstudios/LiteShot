How it works

Three steps.
Nothing to sign up for.

fortefile does the least a server can do: hold scrambled bytes for a while, then forget them.

Step 1

Locked on your device

Your app makes a fresh random key and encrypts the file with AES-256-GCM before anything is uploaded. Only scrambled bytes leave your computer.

Step 2

Shared as one link

The upload gets a long random address, and the key is added after the #. Send the whole link to whoever should see the file.

Step 3

Opened, then gone

The recipient's browser downloads the scrambled file and unlocks it with the key from the link. After 24 hours (or 1 or 12, if you chose), it's deleted for good.

Privacy

We can't see your files.
That's the point.

Here is everything fortefile knows about an upload, and everything it doesn't.

What we can't see

  • Your fileWe only ever receive encrypted bytes.
  • The keyIt stays in the link, after the #, which browsers don't send to servers.
  • Names and accountsFile names aren't uploaded, and there are no accounts to make.

What we do see

  • Size and expiryHow big the encrypted file is, and when it will be deleted.
  • A network addressLike any website, we see the IP address an upload comes from and use it only to limit abuse. It isn't stored with your file.
  • A scrambled delete codeSo the uploader, and only the uploader, can delete the file early.

One exception: search similar images. When you ask LiteShot to search for similar images, that screenshot is uploaded unencrypted so Google Lens can fetch it, and it's deleted within an hour. Everything else is encrypted.

Questions

What can you see?

Only scrambled data, how big it is, and when it expires. The key that unlocks it is the part of the link after the #. Browsers keep that part to themselves, so it never reaches fortefile, and we couldn't open your file even if we were asked to.

How long do files last?

24 hours at most. Whoever uploads can choose 1 or 12 hours instead. When time is up, the file is deleted from our storage and the link stops working.

Can I delete a file sooner?

Yes. The app that uploaded it keeps a private delete code. In LiteShot, delete the upload from the tray menu and it's gone right away, for everyone.

What if I lose the link?

Then nobody can open the file, including us. We never have the key, so there's nothing to recover. Upload the file again and share the new link.

Who can open my link?

Anyone who has the whole link, until it expires. Share it only with people you're happy to show the file to.

How do I report a link?

Email abuse@fortefile.com with the whole link, including the part after the #, so we can see what it is and remove it. Our Acceptable Use Policy explains what happens next.

What can I share?

Files up to 25 MB. Screenshots, images and short videos open right in the browser; anything else can be downloaded.
